UdharGuru Privacy Policy

How UdharGuru protects your business records, safeguards customer credit data, and limits third-party cloud infrastructure liability.

🔒

1. Our 100% Data Security Commitment

At UdharGuru, we treat the confidentiality and integrity of your store's financial records as our highest priority. Your digital ledgers, customer contact lists, transaction vouchers, bill histories, and expense records are processed using industry-standard TLS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.

🛡️ Bank-Grade Standard: All financial ledger entries are kept confidential and restricted strictly to your authenticated account.
📥

2. Information Collection & Usage

We collect only the essential information necessary to provide seamless bookkeeping, interest calculations, and cloud backup services:

  • Authentication Credentials: Your merchant name, phone number, and Google email account, utilized solely for identity verification via Google Sign-In and Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Ledger & Financial Entries: Customer profiles, credit (Udhar) and debit entries, transaction timestamps, payment notes, and automated interest terms you configure.
  • Offline Hive Database & Cloud Sync: All records are written instantaneously to your device's local binary cache (Hive) for zero-latency offline operation, and synced with secure Google Firebase Firestore cloud servers when internet access is available.
  • Zero Data Selling: We NEVER sell, lease, rent, or trade your personal, customer, or transaction data to third-party ad networks, marketing brokers, or data aggregators.
⚠️

3. Limitation of Liability for Cloud Infrastructure & Data Loss

While UdharGuru employs industry-standard Google Cloud and Firebase infrastructure for remote backup synchronization, UdharGuru, Guruji Tech Point, and its developer team shall not be held liable, legally accountable, or financially responsible for any loss, corruption, unauthorized access, or temporary unavailability of records resulting from:

  • Third-party cloud infrastructure downtime, server errors, upstream network outages, or Google Cloud platform service disruptions.
  • User device failure, hardware malfunction, phone loss or theft, factory reset, operating system corruption, or malware on the user's phone.
  • Accidental record deletion performed by the user or failure to maintain regular offline backups.
💡 Best Practice for Merchants: Users are strongly advised to periodically export an offline JSON data backup via Settings → Backup & Data → Export JSON Backup to maintain an independent local copy of their business ledgers.
👤

4. Data Ownership & User Rights

You retain full, exclusive ownership of your business records at all times. Within the UdharGuru app, you have complete authority over your store's information:

  • Modify & Correct: Update, edit, or adjust customer contact numbers, credit entries, debit vouchers, and interest parameters at any point.
  • Data Export & Portability: Export complete ledger reports and customer account statements as clean PDF summaries to share via WhatsApp or save locally.
  • Recycle Bin Management: Deleted customer entries are held in the in-app Recycle Bin for quick recovery before permanent clearance.
🗑️
