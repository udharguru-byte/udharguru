What is UdharGuru & Why Was It Created?

For generations, Indian retailers, kirana shopkeepers, and wholesalers have relied on paper notebooks (Bahi-Khata) to track customer credit. But paper registers get torn, pages get lost, ink fades, and calculating monthly compound interest by hand causes awkward disputes and financial losses.

UdharGuru was created to solve this forever. It is an all-in-one, ultra-fast mobile application that turns your smartphone into a smart digital ledger. Designed with love in India, UdharGuru works 100% offline, requires zero accounting training, and keeps every single rupee safe.
