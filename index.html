About UdharGuru

Version 2.9.5  ·  Build 130  ·  Updated October 2026

App Version: 2.9.5+130

Active Merchants: 10,000+ Stores

Play Store Rating: 4.8 / 5.0 ⭐

Platform: Flutter, Hive Local DB & Firebase

UdharGuru v2.9.5 is an enterprise-grade digital business management app designed for retail shops, wholesalers, and service providers. It combines Customer Credit Ledger (Khata), Automatic Interest Engine (Byaj), Bill/Invoice Generator, Shop Expense Manager, Inventory Stock Tracking, and 1-Click WhatsApp PDF/Receipt Sharing into one simple, free tool.

What's New in Version 2.9.5

  • Automated Byaj Engine: Set monthly interest percentage rates (%) on credit loans. Automatic monthly additions for both Simple and Compound Interest.
  • Bluetooth Thermal POS Printing: Wirelessly connect any 58mm or 80mm ESC/POS Thermal Receipt printer to print bills on the spot.
  • NovaPay Seamless Gateway: Automated online UPI payments integration for Pro plan activations.
  • Itemized Bill & Tax Invoice Creator: Create professional GST/tax bills, apply discounts, and generate instant PDFs.
  • 1-Click WhatsApp Sharing: Send payment reminders, image receipts, and PDF statements to customer WhatsApp in one single click.
  • Shop Expense Manager (Kharacha Book): Record daily operational shop expenses (Rent, Electricity, Goods, Salaries) with visual category charts.
  • Inventory & Stock Tracking: Maintain product catalog, set unit prices, track available quantities, and receive Low Stock alerts.
  • Multi-Business Profiles: Manage multiple shops, branches or business profiles seamlessly from a single login.
  • Biometric & PIN App Lock: Protect your financial data using Fingerprint / Face ID / PIN code security lock.
  • JSON Full Backup & 5-Day Recycle Bin: Safely recover deleted records or export offline JSON backups anytime.

Our Mission

To eliminate manual paper registers for every small shopkeeper in India by offering a 100% free, offline-ready, bank-grade secure digital business management system.

Detailed Module Guide

1. Customer Khata & Phonetic Dedup

Add customers, record credit given (Udhar / Lena Hai) and payments received (Jama / Dena Hai) with photo bill attachments and custom dates.

Example: Add "Raju Kumar" → Try adding "raju kumar"
App Response: "Customer already exists: Raju Kumar" ✅

2. Automatic Interest (Byaj) Engine

Enable Interest on any credit transaction, set the monthly interest percentage (e.g. 2% or 3%), and choose Simple or Compound interest mode. The background engine calculates and posts monthly interest transactions automatically on due dates.

3. Bill Generator & Bluetooth Thermal Printing

Select products from inventory or enter custom items to generate instant PDF bills. Connect any standard Bluetooth Thermal Printer (POS) to print physical receipts on the spot.

4. 1-Click WhatsApp PDF & Receipt Share

Send instant PDF account statements, payment reminder alerts, or digital bill receipts directly to your customer's WhatsApp in just one tap.

5. Expense Manager (Kharacha Book)

Track business expenses separately from customer ledgers. Group expenses by category (Shop Rent, Utility Bills, Salaries, Stock Purchase) and view monthly P&L summaries.

6. Security & Offline Architecture

  • Biometric Lock: Enable Fingerprint, Face ID, or App PIN from Settings.
  • Offline Architecture: Ultra-fast Hive local database ensures zero lag offline.
  • Cloud Backup: Automatically backs up to encrypted Firebase cloud when connected.
  • Recycle Bin: Deleted data remains safely in the Recycle Bin for 5 days.

Privacy Policy

Version 2.9.5  ·  Effective October 2026

Your business data belongs strictly to you. UdharGuru processes your records encrypted under your personal account and never sells, rents, or monetizes user information to third-party ad networks.

What Information We Collect

  • Account Authentication: Mobile phone number, display name, and Google email used for OAuth & Firebase authentication.
  • Ledger & Financial Data: Customer names, credit/debit entries, interest rates, notes, transaction dates, and attached receipt photos.
  • Billing & Inventory Data: Item names, prices, stock quantities, and invoice records generated in the app.
  • Expense Records: Categories, amounts, and notes entered in Expense Manager.
  • Bluetooth & Printer Info: Used solely to discover and connect local Bluetooth thermal receipt printers.

How Your Data Is Protected (100% Data Security)

  • Encryption in Transit & At Rest: SSL/TLS 1.3 network encryption and encrypted Firebase Cloud Firestore storage (AES-256).
  • Biometric App Lock: Local device authentication (Fingerprint / Face / PIN) keeps local data safe from unauthorized physical access.
  • No Third-Party Data Sharing: Your ledgers, customer details, and sales figures are strictly confidential.

Limitation of Liability for Cloud Infrastructure & Data Loss

UdharGuru utilizes industry-leading Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure for cloud hosting and synchronization. However, UdharGuru and its developers shall not be held liable or legally responsible for any data loss, data corruption, or server downtime resulting from:

  • Third-party cloud infrastructure failure, server downtime, or Google service outages.
  • Device failure, operating system corruption, or unauthorized device access.
  • User accidental deletion or failure to maintain regular backups.

Your Data Control & Independent Backup

  • JSON Backup & Restore: Export & import 100% of your data as a JSON file anytime via Settings -> Backup & Data for independent offline preservation.
  • Instant Export: Export complete ledger and expense statements to PDF.
  • Recycle Bin: Soft-deleted customers, transactions, and expenses can be recovered from the Recycle Bin within 5 days.
  • Permanent Account Deletion: Contact support at support@udharguru.in to erase your account permanently.
